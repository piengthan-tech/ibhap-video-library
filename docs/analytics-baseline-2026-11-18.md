## Interpretation

The sample is too small to judge content performance, acquisition quality, or user preference. The current numbers are useful only as a technical baseline confirming that PMNapan Library page views, sessions and at least one outbound-source click are reaching the connected GA4 property.

All observed acquisition in this baseline is direct traffic. No conclusion about search, social, referral or campaign performance should be made from this sample.
